🧠 How to Talk to an NLP Bot

Our bot uses statistical mathematics (TF-IDF) rather than a Generative AI like ChatGPT. It doesn't "read" English; it calculates the frequency of important keywords. Here is how to format your questions to get the perfect match!

🗣️ The Rambler

Messy Input: "Ummm hi bot, can u plz tell me where my package is???"

How NLP sees it (TF-IDF):
ummmhibot tellpackage

The NLP engine drops conversational filler. If you type too much fluff, it dilutes the weight of the important words.

✅ Better Input: "Where is my package?"

📖 The Over-Explainer

Messy Input: "I ordered a blender yesterday for my mom's birthday but it arrived broken in half."

How NLP sees it (TF-IDF):
mombirthdayyesterday arrivedbroken

Adding context ("mom's birthday") introduces vocabulary that the FAQ database doesn't care about, confusing the similarity calculation.

✅ Better Input: "Item arrived broken or damaged."

😎 The Slang User

Messy Input: "Yo, how do I get my moolah back for this?"

How NLP sees it (TF-IDF):
yomoolah

Our dataset vocabulary is trained on standard Amazon FAQs. It won't recognize slang words like "moolah".

✅ Better Input: "How do I get a refund?"

🌫️ The Vague Question

Messy Input: "It doesn't work."

How NLP sees it (TF-IDF):
work

Too generic! The word "work" might match with hundreds of FAQs. Give the engine specific keywords to anchor onto.

✅ Better Input: "Machine is not turning on."

🔀 The Multi-Question

Messy Input: "Do you ship to Canada and how much is shipping and how long does it take?"

How NLP sees it (TF-IDF):
shipcanadacostlong

Multiple distinct topics pull the Cosine Similarity vector in different directions, resulting in a low confidence score across all of them.

✅ Better Input: "Do you ship to Canada?" (Ask one at a time)
